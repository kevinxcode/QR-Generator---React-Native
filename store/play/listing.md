## Full description
Qraft is a modern QR code and barcode studio that works completely offline.

SCAN ANYTHING
• QR codes, EAN-13/8, UPC-A/E, Code 128/39/93, ITF, Codabar, Data Matrix, PDF417 and Aztec
• Smart actions: join Wi-Fi details, save contacts, call, email, open maps, add events
• Scan from photos in your gallery
• Batch mode for inventory and events, with duplicate detection and CSV export
• Link safety: see the real domain before opening, with warnings for unusual links

DESIGN STUNNING QR CODES
• 17 content types: website, Wi-Fi, contact card, WhatsApp, email, SMS, location, event and more
• Body patterns, corner styles, gradients and colour palettes
• Add your logo, frames and call-to-action labels
• Built-in scannability score so your design stays reliable
• Export PNG (up to 4096 px), SVG or PDF

BARCODES DONE RIGHT
• Check digits validated automatically for EAN and UPC
• Custom colours, text and margins

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no cloud, no tracking
• Your history, favourites, folders and tags stay on your device
• Local backup and restore
