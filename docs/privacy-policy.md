# Qraft Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Qraft ("the app") is a QR code and barcode scanner and generator for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. In short: Qraft works offline, has no accounts, and does not collect, sell or share your personal data.

## Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Qraft has no sign-up, login or user profile, and it does not use analytics, advertising, advertising identifiers or third-party tracking tools.

## Data stored on your device

Your scan history, generated codes, designs, collections and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This data is never uploaded to us or to any server. You can delete it at any time from Settings > Manage & delete data, and uninstalling the app removes all of it. If you use the Backup feature, the backup file is saved wherever you choose, and you are in control of it.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: used only to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are processed on your device and are never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
- **Photos and media**: used only when you choose an image to scan a code from, add a logo to a QR code, or save an exported code to your gallery. The app does not read the location data in your photos.
- **Vibration**: used for haptic feedback when a code is scanned.

Qraft does not request microphone, location or contacts permissions.

## Clipboard

The app reads your clipboard only when you tap "Create from clipboard".

## Links and sharing

Qraft does not open scanned links automatically unless you turn on that setting. When you open a link, search the web, or share a file, that content goes to the app or website you choose, and their privacy policy applies from that point.

## App updates

To deliver bug fixes, the app may check the Expo update service (expo.dev) for new versions. This check sends only technical information needed to deliver the update, such as the app version and platform. It does not include your scan history or any content you create.

## Children's privacy

Qraft does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
